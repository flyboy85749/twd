For a local business, search engine optimization (SEO) mostly comes down to three things: a complete Google Business Profile, a fast site that clearly says what you do and where, and steady reviews.

## What I do

- Set up or clean up your Google Business Profile, including categories, service area, hours, and photos
- Write page titles and descriptions that match what Tucson customers actually search for
- Add structured business data (schema.org) so Google and AI assistants can read your address, hours, and services
- Make sure AI crawlers can reach your site, and add an `llms.txt` file that summarizes your business for them
- Fix technical problems: slow pages, broken links, and missing sitemaps

## What I won't do

I don't sell link schemes, fake reviews, or "page one guaranteed" packages. Those can get a business penalized. The work above is steady and honest, and it compounds over time.
