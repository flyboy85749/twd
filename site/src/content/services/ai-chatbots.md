How many times a week do you answer "Are you open Saturday?" or "Do you come out to Marana?" A chatbot trained on your own information can answer those instantly, in your tone, even at 10 p.m.

## How it works

1. We list the questions customers actually ask, along with your answers.
2. I build an assistant that answers only from that information and doesn't make things up about your business.
3. When it can't answer, or someone wants a quote, it collects their contact info and emails it to you.
4. You get a simple dashboard showing what people ask, which is also useful for improving your site.

The chatbot sits on your website as a small chat bubble. Premium plans can also answer in Spanish and connect to your CRM.
