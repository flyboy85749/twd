The business that answers first usually gets the job. If a lead fills out your form while you're on a ladder, an automated text and email can reply in under a minute and offer a time to talk.

## Common workflows

- **Instant lead reply:** a text and an email go out the moment someone submits your form.
- **Missed-call text-back:** if you can't pick up, the caller gets a text so they don't call your competitor.
- **Booking confirmations and reminders** that cut down on no-shows
- **Quote follow-ups** a few days after you send an estimate
- **Review requests** after a job is marked complete

I build these in n8n, an automation platform you can own and self-host, so you aren't locked into another per-seat subscription. Every workflow comes with written documentation.
