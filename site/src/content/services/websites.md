Most small business sites have one job: turn someone searching on their phone into a phone call, a form, or a visit. I build sites around that job instead of around a template.

## What you get

- A site you own outright, including the code, domain, and content
- Pages that load in about a second on a phone
- Clear calls to action on every page: call, book, or get a quote
- Built-in basics for Google: page titles, a sitemap, and business details search engines can read
- Accessible design that meets WCAG AA, so everyone can use it

## How it works

1. **A free 30-minute call.** We talk about your customers, what they ask, and what you want the site to do.
2. **A fixed quote.** You get a written price and timeline before any work starts.
3. **Design and build.** You see real pages early and give feedback in rounds.
4. **Launch and handoff.** I set up hosting, connect your domain, and show you how to make simple edits.

Already have a site you're unhappy with? I also redesign and migrate existing sites, including WordPress.
