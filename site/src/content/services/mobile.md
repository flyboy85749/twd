More than half of local searches happen on a phone, often from a parking lot or a job site. If your site makes people pinch and zoom, they go back to the search results and call the next business.

## What mobile-first means here

- Tap-to-call and tap-for-directions buttons that are easy to reach
- Text that's readable without zooming
- Forms that are short and use the right phone keyboard for each field
- Images sized for phones, so pages load quickly on cellular data

Every site I build is tested on real phones and tablets before launch. If you already have a site, I can make it work properly on phones without starting over.
