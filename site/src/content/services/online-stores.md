You don't need a huge e-commerce platform to take money online. Many local businesses just need customers to pay a deposit, buy a gift card, or book a time slot.

## Options I set up

- **Stripe or Square checkout** for deposits, invoices, and simple product sales
- **Shopify** when you have a real catalog with inventory and shipping
- **Calendly or Square Appointments** for booking, with automatic reminders
- **Gift cards and service packages** for salons, studios, and restaurants

I'll recommend the simplest setup that does what you need, so you aren't paying monthly for features you never use.
