For most local businesses, the Google Business Profile, the box with your map pin, hours, and reviews, brings in more calls than the website does. It's free, and most profiles are half finished.

Open your profile at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and work through this list.

## The checklist

1. **Primary category.** Pick the most specific one that fits ("HVAC contractor," not "Contractor"). This matters more than anything else on the list.
2. **Secondary categories.** Add up to nine more for the other services you actually offer.
3. **Service area.** If you go to customers, list the areas you cover: Tucson, Oro Valley, Marana, Vail, Sahuarita, and so on.
4. **Hours, including holiday hours.** Wrong hours lead to one-star reviews.
5. **Phone and website.** Use the same phone number that's on your website, written the same way.
6. **Services list.** Add each service with a one-sentence description.
7. **Photos.** Add at least 10 real photos of your work, your team, and your storefront or truck. Skip stock photos.
8. **Business description.** Write two or three plain sentences about what you do and who you serve.
9. **Ask for reviews.** Copy your review link and send it to your last five happy customers today.
10. **Answer reviews.** Reply to every review, good or bad, briefly and politely.

## Keep it alive

Post an update or a photo once or twice a month. A profile that looks active gets more clicks than one that hasn't changed since 2019.
