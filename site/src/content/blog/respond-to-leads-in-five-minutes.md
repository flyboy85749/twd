Picture someone whose AC died in July. They search, find three companies, and fill out all three contact forms. Whoever calls back first usually gets the job, and a reply two hours later often reaches someone who has already booked.

## Why speed wins

People contacting a local service business usually have a problem right now. The first business that responds sets the expectations, gives a price range, and books the visit. The others are competing for a job that's already gone.

## Ways to respond faster without hiring

1. **Auto-reply by text and email.** The moment a form comes in, send a friendly message: "Thanks, this is Mike with Sonoran Plumbing. I'm on a job and will call you within the hour. Want to grab a time now?" with a booking link.
2. **Missed-call text-back.** If you can't answer, the caller gets an automatic text so they don't move on to the next number.
3. **A chatbot for the easy questions.** Hours, service area, and "Do you work on tankless water heaters?" can be answered instantly, any time of day.
4. **One inbox.** Send form leads, texts, and chatbot handoffs to the same place so nothing slips through.

## What this costs

A basic instant-reply workflow usually takes a few days to set up and runs for a small monthly fee. Compared with the value of one lost job, it's one of the cheapest upgrades a service business can make.
