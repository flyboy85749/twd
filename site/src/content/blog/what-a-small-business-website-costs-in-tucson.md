"How much does a website cost?" is like asking how much a truck costs. It depends on what you need it to haul. Here are honest ranges for Tucson small businesses, and what moves the price.

## Typical ranges

- **Do-it-yourself builders (Wix, Squarespace):** $20–$50 a month, plus your time. Fine for getting started, though they're often slow and hard to rank.
- **A simple professional site (about 5 pages):** $800–$1,500. Home, services, about, gallery, and contact.
- **A growing business site (about 10 pages):** $1,500–$3,000. Adds online booking or payments, a blog, a team page, and more SEO work.
- **Custom features:** $3,500 and up. Customer portals, quote calculators, multiple locations.

You can see exactly where my packages fall on the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## What drives the cost up

1. **Custom design versus a proven layout.** A layout built from your branding takes less time than one designed from a blank page.
2. **Writing the content.** If you have your text and photos ready, the project goes much faster.
3. **Integrations.** Each connection to payments, booking, or a CRM adds setup and testing.
4. **Revision rounds.** Agreeing on scope up front keeps the price fixed.

## Costs people forget

- **Domain name:** about $15–$25 a year
- **Hosting:** $0–$25 a month for most small sites
- **Maintenance:** security updates and small edits, if you don't want to handle them yourself
- **Photos:** a half-day with a local photographer often does more for your site than any design change

## The question to ask instead

Ask "What does one new customer a month cost me today?" If a site brings in even one extra job a month, a $1,800 site usually pays for itself within the first year.
